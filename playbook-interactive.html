🎯 PBA Partner Engagement Playbook

Your complete framework for driving pipeline visibility and partner success

🎯 Your Role & Responsibilities

  • Own the partner relationship — You are the strategic point of contact for pipeline visibility and growth
  • Drive pipeline visibility — Ensure partners commit to forecasts and share deal details
  • Identify co-sell opportunities — Find where we can partner to win deals together
  • Ensure accountability — Partners follow through on commitments; you follow through on support

💼 What Success Looks Like

You've built trust with partners. They proactively share pipeline, ask for your help, and credit you with wins. Your team sees consistent, committed pipeline from every partner account.

📊 How You're Measured

📞 Minimum 1 Strategic Call/Partner/Month
Regular touchpoints. Quarterly QBRs + monthly check-ins minimum. Non-negotiable.
💰 Pipeline Visibility: $X Committed by Each Partner
Each partner commits to pipeline numbers. You track and report quarterly.
🤝 Co-Sell Opportunities: Y Deals Identified/Quarter
Minimum 2-3 co-sell opportunities per partner per year. Logged and tracked.
😊 Partner Satisfaction: Partners Feel Supported & Aligned
Partners rate you as responsive, knowledgeable, and invested in their success.

📅 Engagement Cadence

Q
Quarterly: Strategic Business Review (QBR)

Full pipeline review. Growth goals. Co-sell planning. Market trends. 60-90 min call.

M
Monthly: Check-In Call

Pipeline updates. Risks & blockers. Co-sell progress. 30-45 min call.

A
As-Needed: Deal-Specific Calls

Co-sell opportunities. At-risk deal recovery. Executive escalation. 15-30 min focused calls.

📋 Before Every Call: Pre-Call Prep

Do this 24 hours before the call. Takes 30 minutes.

1
Research partner's current pipeline
Pull last forecast shared. What deals are they working? What's the sizing? What changed since last call?
2
Identify 2-3 co-sell opportunities or gaps
Where can you add value? What are they missing? What can you help with?
3
Prepare specific pipeline questions
Write them down. Example: "Walk me through your Q3 forecast... what deals are at risk? What's new?"
4
Send agenda to partner (sets expectations)
Subject: "QBR Agenda - June 15th at 10am". Takes 2 minutes. Shows you're organized.

🎯 During the Call: What You Must Cover

Use the Partner Engagement Checklist to track these items in real-time.

1
Partner's Q1-Q4 pipeline and growth goals
Listen first. Ask: "What are your top priorities for this year? How can we help you hit your targets?"
2
Your role in their top 3-5 deals (co-sell opportunities)
Be specific. "I see a fit here. Can we work together on this deal?"
3
Any at-risk deals or blockers
Ask: "Are there any deals that are at risk or stalled? How can I help unblock?"
4
Commitments: What they will share, when you'll follow up
Get explicit. "By next Friday, can you send me the detailed forecast? I'll send co-sell details by Tuesday."

📧 After Every Call: Post-Call Execution

Do this within 24 hours. This is where the real work happens.

1
Send recap with pipeline summary and co-sell details
Use the post-call email template. Include: what was discussed, deals identified, next steps, your commitment.
2
Log deals into SharePoint List
Partner name, opportunity, stage, sizing, close date. This is your pipeline record.
3
Schedule next call (calendar invite)
Don't wait. Send invite same day. "Next monthly check-in: July 15th at 10am."
4
Flag any risks to leadership
Partner at risk? Pipeline concerns? Send Slack to your manager: "Partner X flagged $2M deal at risk. Needs executive help."
5
Update CRM with new information
Add notes, update deal stages, document commitments. Your manager and team need visibility.

🚩 Red Flags: Escalate Immediately

If you see any of these, alert your manager TODAY. Don't wait.

🚩 Partner hasn't committed pipeline in 30+ days
This is a relationship risk. Schedule a call. If they ghost you, escalate.
🚩 Partner deprioritizing a key deal
Customer put deal on hold? Budget got cut? You need to know NOW to help find alternatives.
🚩 Competitor gaining ground with partner
Partner mentioned they're exploring other solutions. This is your signal to increase engagement.
🚩 Partner dissatisfied or unresponsive
Missing calls? Short answers? Unhappy tone? This relationship is cooling. Escalate to leadership.

✅ When in doubt, escalate. Your job is to catch problems early. Better to over-communicate than miss a risk.
